GALACREDIT USER AGREEMENT

Last updated: 18 August 2026

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Please read this agreement carefully before using GalaCredit. By signing in, submitting an application or accepting a loan offer, you confirm that you understand and agree to these terms.

This document is an operational draft for the Ghana service and must be reviewed by qualified Ghanaian legal and compliance advisers before production release.

1. SERVICE

GalaCredit provides an online application service for short-term cash loans. Applications, credit decisions, fees, disbursements, repayment schedules and account status may be displayed through the GalaCredit application.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. Approval, the available amount and the applicable terms depend on eligibility, risk assessment and current product settings.

2. ELIGIBILITY

You must be legally capable of entering into a credit agreement in Ghana and must provide accurate, complete and current information. You may not apply on behalf of another person or use another person's identity, mobile number, Ghana Card or MoMo account.

3. ACCOUNT ACCESS

Your registered mobile number and verification code are used to access your account. You are responsible for protecting your mobile device, SIM card and verification codes. Contact customer support immediately if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

4. APPLICATION INFORMATION

You authorise GalaCredit and its approved service providers to verify information submitted during the application process, including identity, contact, device, location and risk information, subject to applicable law and the Personal Data Authorization.

Where the platform shows a device-risk consent dialog, you also understand that the service may request permission to collect SMS content, installed app names and a device fingerprint summary through an approved mobile app bridge or other supported integration. If the device or browser does not support those permissions, the service may still use the other information you submit and the device signals it can lawfully obtain.

False, misleading or incomplete information may result in rejection, suspension, collection action or reporting where permitted by law.

5. LOAN TERMS

Before confirming a loan, the application will display the nominal loan amount, upfront fees, estimated cash received, repayment amount, repayment dates, instalments and overdue charges.

Upfront fees may be deducted before disbursement. The amount sent to your MoMo wallet may therefore be lower than the nominal loan amount. Your repayment obligation is the amount shown in the confirmed repayment schedule.

Product settings may change for future applications. Terms saved with an accepted loan remain the record for that loan unless changed by a lawful written agreement.

6. MOMO DISBURSEMENT AND REPAYMENT

Approved funds may be sent through a third-party mobile money provider. You must ensure that the registered mobile number and MoMo account are active and controlled by you.

Repayments must be made only through payment methods displayed or confirmed by GalaCredit. Never transfer repayment funds to a private account or an account provided through an unofficial message.

7. LATE PAYMENT

If you do not repay by the scheduled due date, overdue charges and collection activity may apply as disclosed before confirmation and as permitted by law. Contact customer support promptly if you expect difficulty making a repayment.

8. COMMUNICATIONS

You agree to receive service messages relating to verification, application status, disbursement, repayment, security and customer support through SMS, phone, in-app notices or other approved channels.

9. RESPONSIBLE USE

Borrow only what you can repay. You must not use the service for fraud, money laundering, unlawful activity, identity misuse, account resale or attempts to bypass security and risk controls.

You agree that risk controls may analyse permitted device, SMS and app-list signals together with application history and other lawful risk factors to detect borrowing pressure, device abuse, fraud or other material default indicators.

10. PRIVACY

Personal data is handled according to the Personal Data Authorization, applicable privacy notices and Ghanaian law. The service uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but you must also protect your device and account access.

11. SERVICE AVAILABILITY

The application may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, network failure, MoMo provider issues or events outside reasonable control. GalaCredit will take reasonable steps to restore affected services.

12. COMPLAINTS AND SUPPORT

Questions, complaints and repayment support requests may be submitted through the Customer Support page. Keep copies of payment confirmations and relevant communications.

13. CHANGES

These terms may be updated for future use of the service. Material changes will be communicated through the application or another appropriate channel. Changes will not rewrite an existing accepted loan unless permitted by law and agreed through an appropriate process.

14. GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is intended to be governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana. Any mandatory consumer protection, credit, data protection and dispute resolution requirements remain applicable.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read, understood and accepted this User Agreement.
